About the job

Kestrel Pay is a Series C fintech building the payments infrastructure that 4,000+ B2B SaaS platforms embed to accept and route payments. We're hiring a Senior Product Manager, Platform to own our developer platform: public APIs, SDKs, webhooks, and the partner onboarding experience.

What you'll do
- Own the product strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for the Developer Platform, aligned to company OKRs.
- Lead discovery with developers, partners, and internal stakeholders; translate insights into clear PRDs and user stories.
- Drive API design decisions with engineering (REST, versioning, idempotency, rate limiting) and improve developer experience (DX) across docs, SDKs, and sandbox environments.
- Reduce partner time-to-first-transaction by redesigning self-serve onboarding and activation flows.
- Define and track success metrics (activation rate, API adoption, retention) using SQL and product analytics tools such as Amplitude or Looker; run A/B tests to validate changes.
- Partner with Go-to-Market, Solutions Engineering and Customer Success on launches, pricing and packaging, and enablement.
- Manage cross-functional stakeholder alignment across engineering, design, compliance and risk.
- Mentor junior PMs and contribute to product operations best practices.

What you'll bring
- 7+ years of product management experience, including 3+ years in technical product management for APIs or developer platforms in B2B SaaS.
- Proven track record shipping 0-to-1 products and scaling them.
- Strong data fluency: comfortable writing SQL and building dashboards.
- Experience with agile delivery (Scrum/Kanban) and tools like Jira or Linear.
- Excellent written communication; you write crisp specs and executive updates.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience.

Nice to have
- Payments, fintech, or PCI DSS compliance experience.
- Experience with usage-based pricing or platform monetization.
- Familiarity with microservices architecture and event-driven systems.

Location: Remote (US). Occasional travel to Denver HQ.
